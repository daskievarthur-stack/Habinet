# Habinet Laws

> Every architectural decision must respect these laws.

---

# Why Laws?

Technology changes.

Frameworks change.

Databases change.

Programming languages change.

These laws should not.

They define the immutable foundation of Habinet.

---

# Law 1 — Everything begins as a Grain

There is only one primary entity in Habinet.

Everything else is built from it.

---

# Law 2 — Knowledge grows

Knowledge is not copied.

Knowledge evolves.

---

# Law 3 — Context is part of knowledge

No Grain exists in isolation.

Context is intrinsic.

---

# Law 4 — Relations create meaning

A Grain gains value through its relationships.

Connections are first-class citizens.

---

# Law 5 — History is never lost

Every change contributes to understanding.

History is part of the knowledge itself.

---

# Law 6 — Views do not define reality

Mail, calendar, document and chat are different views of the same underlying knowledge.

---

# Law 7 — Capabilities over types

A Grain is not limited by a fixed type.

Behavior emerges from capabilities.

---

# Law 8 — AI understands context

Artificial Intelligence works with the same knowledge graph as the user.

It is not an isolated feature.

---

# Law 9 — Simplicity is a decision

Every new capability must reduce complexity instead of increasing it.

---

# Law 10 — Habinet stores knowledge, not files

Files are representations.

Knowledge is the foundation.
