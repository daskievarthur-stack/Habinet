# Habinet Glossary

This document defines the core language of Habinet.

Every architectural decision, RFC and implementation should use these terms consistently.

---

# Context

The complete network of identities, relationships, history and knowledge surrounding a person, object or event.

Context is the primary concept of Habinet.

---

# Entity

Anything that exists inside Habinet.

People.

Documents.

Messages.

Ideas.

Tasks.

Events.

Files.

Projects.

Everything is an entity.

---

# Identity

The permanent identity of an entity.

Identity never changes during the lifetime of an entity.

---

# Timeline

The complete history of an entity.

Every meaningful change becomes part of its timeline.

History is never detached from identity.

---

# Relation

A semantic connection between two entities.

Relations describe how entities are connected.

---

# Event

A recorded occurrence that changes the state of one or more entities.

Events become part of history.

---

# Capability

A behavior or ability that can be attached to an entity.

Capabilities define what an entity can do.

---

# Grain

The smallest meaningful unit of knowledge inside Habinet.

A grain represents a single piece of information that can participate in relationships, history and context.

---

# Graph

The complete network formed by entities and their relationships.

The graph represents knowledge, not storage.

---

# Personal Context

The complete digital context of a person.

It includes identities, history, documents, conversations, relationships and knowledge.

Personal Context belongs to the person, not to the device or platform.
