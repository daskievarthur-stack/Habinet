# Habinet Manifesto

> *Everything has context.*

---

## Why

Software has transformed the way we work, communicate, learn and create.

Yet one fundamental problem remains.

Our digital lives are fragmented.

Emails live in one application.
Documents in another.
Conversations somewhere else.
Calendars follow their own timeline.
Artificial intelligence starts every conversation from scratch.

Each application knows only a small part of our world.

None of them understands the whole picture.

We believe this is not a limitation of technology.

It is a limitation of how software has been designed.

Habinet exists because context should not be fragmented.

Context should belong to people.

Not to applications.

Not to devices.

Not to cloud providers.

To people.

---

## The Problem

Modern software treats information as isolated objects.

An email is just an email.

A document is just a document.

A meeting is just a meeting.

A note is just a note.

But reality does not work this way.

Every conversation leads to an idea.

Every idea becomes a document.

Every document creates a task.

Every task belongs to a project.

Everything is connected.

Software should understand these relationships instead of forcing people to recreate them every day.

---

## The Vision

We are not building another application.

We are building a Context Operating System.

A system where every entity has an identity.

Every change has a history.

Every entity can be connected.

Every interaction contributes to a living graph of knowledge.

Applications become different views of the same reality.

Mail.

Calendar.

Documents.

Chat.

Ideas.

AI.

Not separate products.

One connected context.

---

## Our Principles

* Everything has context.
* Context belongs to people, not devices.
* Identity is permanent.
* History matters.
* Relationships are first-class.
* AI should understand the same context as humans.
* Portability is a right, not a feature.
* Simplicity enables longevity.
* Architecture is part of the product.

---

## Our Promise

We will always design Habinet around people.

Your context is yours.

It should remain understandable.

It should remain portable.

It should remain connected.

Always.
