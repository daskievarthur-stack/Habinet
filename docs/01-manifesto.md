# Habinet Manifesto

> We believe knowledge deserves a better home.

---

## The World We Live In

Modern software is fragmented.

We jump between email, chat, documents, calendars, notes, tasks and cloud storage.

Every application solves one problem.

None of them understand the whole picture.

Information becomes disconnected.

Context disappears.

Knowledge is constantly rebuilt instead of continuously growing.

---

## We Believe

Information should not belong to applications.

Applications should exist to serve information.

People should not waste time deciding where something belongs.

Everything belongs to the same knowledge space.

---

## Context Matters

An email is not just an email.

It belongs to a project.

It belongs to a conversation.

It belongs to a decision.

It belongs to people.

Without context, information loses its value.

---

## Simplicity

Complexity is easy to create.

Simplicity requires discipline.

Every feature added to Habinet must make the system simpler,
not more complicated.

If a feature increases complexity without increasing clarity,
it does not belong.

---

## One Foundation

We do not build independent applications.

We build one living system.

Mail.

Calendar.

Documents.

Chat.

Ideas.

Tasks.

Artificial Intelligence.

Different experiences.

One foundation.

---

## Grain

Everything begins as a Grain.

Every Grain can grow.

Every Grain can evolve.

Every Grain can connect to another Grain.

Knowledge is not stored.

Knowledge grows.

---

## Time

Information changes.

People change.

Ideas change.

Habinet remembers.

History is not an optional feature.

History is part of knowledge itself.

---

## Our Responsibility

We are not building software for today.

We are building software that people may trust with years of their lives.

That responsibility affects every decision we make.

---

## Our Promise

We will always choose:

Clarity over complexity.

Systems over features.

Quality over speed.

Long-term thinking over short-term trends.

Knowledge over information.

---

## One Sentence

Habinet is where knowledge grows.
